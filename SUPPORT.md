# Support — JavaScript Challenge

Thanks for playing JavaScript Challenge.

- **Questions, bug reports, feedback:** open an issue in this repository, or contact the developer, Mustafa Gürbüz, at the email address listed on the App Store listing.
- **Restore your purchase:** Settings → "Restore purchases" (sign in with the same Apple Account you bought with).
- **Reset your progress:** Settings → "Reset progress" (this cannot be undone).
- **Privacy:** see [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
