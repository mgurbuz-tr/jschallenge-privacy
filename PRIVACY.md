# Privacy Policy — JavaScript Challenge

_Last updated: September 5, 2026_

## English

**JavaScript Challenge** ("the App") is a single-player, offline coding-practice app for iPhone and iPad, published by Mustafa Gürbüz ("we"). We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

- **No account or sign-in.** The App has no login system and does not know who you are. The optional profile handle you can type in the App is stored only on your device.
- **Your progress stays on your device.** Solved levels, XP, streaks, badges, settings and the code you write are stored locally on your device using the operating system's standard app storage. Nothing is uploaded to any server we control — the App has no server.
- **Code runs on your device.** The JavaScript you write is compiled and executed locally, inside a sandboxed worker in the App. It is never sent anywhere.
- **No analytics, no advertising, no tracking.** The App contains no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or third-party tracking library, and it does not use the advertising identifier.
- **App Store version check.** Once a day at most, the App asks Apple's public App Store lookup service (itunes.apple.com) for the latest published version of the App so it can offer an "update available" prompt. This request contains only the App's bundle identifier; it carries no user identifier and no personal data. If the device is offline the check is skipped silently.
- **In-app purchase.** The App offers one optional one-time purchase ("Full Path") that unlocks the remaining levels. The purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We never see or store your payment details; Apple's own privacy policy governs the transaction. The App only stores, on your device, whether the purchase has been made so the unlocked content stays available. You can restore the purchase on any device signed in with the same Apple Account via "Restore purchases" in Settings.
- **No third parties.** The App does not share any information with third parties, because it does not collect any information to share. Apple, as the operator of the App Store and the payment processor for the in-app purchase, is the only party involved, under Apple's own terms.
- **Children.** Since the App collects no personal data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children either.
- **Deleting your data.** All data the App keeps is on your device. "Reset progress" in Settings erases it, and deleting the App removes it entirely.
- **Changes to this policy.** If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future update adds an online feature), we will update this document and the "Last updated" date above.

**Contact:** if you have any questions about this policy, contact the developer, Mustafa Gürbüz, at the email address listed on the App Store listing.
