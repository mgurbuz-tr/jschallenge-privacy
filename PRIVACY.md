# Privacy Policy — JavaScript Challenge

_Last updated: 6 October 2026_

## English

**JavaScript Challenge** ("the App") is a single-player coding-practice app for iPhone and iPad, published by Mustafa Gürbüz ("we"). We do not collect personal data. From version 1.0.4 the App sends only the anonymous usage statistics described below.

- **No account or sign-in.** The App has no login system and does not know who you are. The optional profile handle you can type in the App is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere.
- **Your progress stays on your device.** Solved levels, XP, streaks, badges, settings and the code you write are stored locally on your device using the operating system's standard app storage. Nothing is uploaded to any server we control — the App has no server.
- **Code runs on your device.** The JavaScript you write is compiled and executed locally, inside a sandboxed worker in the App. Neither your code nor its output is ever sent anywhere.
- **Anonymous usage statistics.** The App sends anonymous usage events to PostHog, an analytics service, so we can see where players get stuck and improve the challenges. Examples: a session started or ended, a screen was opened, a challenge was opened, solved or left unsolved (with the number of runs, the time spent and the number of hints used), a difficulty section was completed, progress was reset, the rating or update prompt was shown or answered. They are used for analytics only. Each event carries a random ID created by the App on your device, the App's version and build number, and your device's language setting. The ID is not your name, contact details, Apple Account, advertising identifier or location, and the events never contain your profile handle, the code you write or its output. The App never links this ID to you (there is no sign-in and no user identification), and our PostHog project is set to discard your IP address and not to derive a location from it. Deleting the App deletes the ID.
- **Technical details added to each event.** The analytics library (posthog-js) automatically adds standard technical details to every event: the device type and model (for example iPhone or iPad), the iOS version, the screen and window size, the device's language setting, the user-agent string of the App's built-in web view (which shows the iOS and web-engine version), the version of the analytics library, and a random ID for the current app session. These details are the same on many devices and are not used to identify you. The App removes the device's time zone, which the library would otherwise add.
- **How long statistics are kept.** The statistics are kept for up to about one year under our analytics plan, after which they may be deleted.
- **Analytics provider.** PostHog, Inc. processes these statistics on our behalf as a data processor, on servers in the United States (us.posthog.com), under its own privacy policy (https://posthog.com/privacy). We do not sell the statistics or share them with anyone else.
- **No advertising, no tracking.** The App contains no advertising SDK, does not use the advertising identifier, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
- **App Store version check.** Once a day at most, the App asks Apple's public App Store lookup service (itunes.apple.com) for the latest published version of the App so it can offer an "update available" prompt. This request contains only the App's bundle identifier; it carries no user identifier and no personal data. If the device is offline the check is skipped silently.
- **Rating prompt.** After you have solved a few challenges, the App may ask in its own short dialog whether you are enjoying it; if you tap "Sure", it asks Apple to show the standard rating prompt. Apple decides whether that prompt appears and handles any rating or review you leave; the App is not told whether or how you rated it.
- **Network.** The App needs no internet connection to work. When one is available it sends only the anonymous statistics and the version check described above.
- **Purchase.** JavaScript Challenge is a paid download on the App Store and contains no in-app purchases. The purchase is processed entirely by Apple; we never see or store your payment details, and Apple's own privacy policy governs the transaction.
- **No other third parties.** Apart from PostHog, which processes the anonymous statistics on our behalf, the App does not share any information with third parties. Apple, as the operator of the App Store, is involved under Apple's own terms.
- **Children.** The App collects no personal data from anyone, so it does not knowingly collect personal data from children either; the anonymous statistics are the same for every user.
- **Deleting your data.** Your progress and code are on your device. "Reset progress" in Settings erases them, and deleting the App removes everything, including the random statistics ID. Statistics already sent are tied only to that random ID and cannot be connected to you.
- **Changes to this policy.** If this policy changes, we will update this document and the "Last updated" date above.

**Contact:** if you have any questions about this policy or the statistics, contact the developer, Mustafa Gürbüz, at mustafagurbuzz0606@gmail.com.

Örnekler: bir oturum başladı veya bitti, bir ekran açıldı, bir soru açıldı, çözüldü veya çözülmeden bırakıldı (çalıştırma sayısı, harcanan süre ve kullanılan ipucu sayısıyla), bir zorluk bölümü tamamlandı, ilerleme sıfırlandı, puanlama veya güncelleme uyarısı gösterildi ya da yanıtlandı. Bu bilgiler pek çok cihazda aynıdır ve sizi tanımlamak için kullanılmaz. Kütüphanenin normalde ekleyeceği saat dilimini Uygulama kaldırır.
